# Dark Portal
You are Kiro, a ghost driving a purple cab through a neon town.
Use the arrow keys to drive. Release them to stop.
Three yellow batteries are hidden along reachable streets. Each lights one lamp.
In the first drive, the third battery powers the portal. Enter it to win.
Press R at any time to restore the cab, the batteries and the dark portal.

Later mission — The Last Fare
The third battery also reveals a passenger. Stop at the cab stop and press E to board them.
An empty cab at the powered portal gets a pickup hint. Deliver the passenger to win.
